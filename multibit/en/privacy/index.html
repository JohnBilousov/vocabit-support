Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

Multibit is an app where children learn math: the times tables, the order of operations, fractions and percentages. We built it to know nothing about you that it doesn't need. This policy explains how the app handles information.

1. What data we collect

None. Multibit has no sign-up, accounts, ads, analytics or third-party tracking. The app sends nothing to any server and works offline.

2. What is stored on the device

Learning progress — completed levels, stars, the daily streak, statistics for examples and topics, records and settings (sounds, vibration, daily goal, language) — is stored only on your device and never shared. You can erase it in the app's settings (“Reset progress”) or by deleting the app.

3. Purchases

There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

4. Children

The app is made for children. Since we collect no personal data, no information about a child ever reaches us.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

6. Contact

Privacy questions: ultimate.solutions@icloud.com
